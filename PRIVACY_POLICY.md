# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 14, 2024

## 1. Data Collection

DevPulse collects metadata regarding API usage, including token counts, endpoint addresses, and timestamps. We do not store the content of your prompts or LLM responses unless specifically configured for debugging.

## 2. Data Usage

Data is used solely to provide security analytics, cost forecasting, and anomaly detection. We do not use your data for any advertising or promotional purposes.

## 3. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. All data is stored in secure, access-controlled environments.

## 4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell your data to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for industry benchmarking purposes only.

## 5. User Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting support. We will respond to such requests within 30 days.

## 6. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact: privacy@devpulse.io
